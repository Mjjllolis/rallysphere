Child Safety Standards

Our standards and practices for preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) on RallySphere.

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Our commitment

RallySphere is a platform for clubs and organizations to run events, sell tickets, and connect with their members. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation of any kind. Content, conduct, or accounts that sexualize, endanger, or exploit minors are prohibited without exception, and we act on credible reports regardless of where they originate.

What is prohibited

The following are banned on RallySphere and result in immediate action:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in any form, including images, video, text, or links.
  • Sexualized depictions or descriptions of minors, whether real, illustrated, or generated.
  • Grooming, sextortion, or any attempt to solicit, coerce, or arrange sexual contact with a minor.
  • Using events, clubs, messages, or profiles to make contact with minors for sexual purposes.
  • Trafficking, advertising, or facilitating the exploitation of a minor.
  • Sharing links or off-platform contact details for the purpose of any of the above.

How to report a concern

Anyone can report a child safety concern directly inside the RallySphere app. Every event, club, and profile has a Report option in its menu; choose Child safety as the reason. Reports filed under that category are escalated for immediate review rather than entering the normal queue.

You can also block any user from the same menu, which hides their events and clubs from your feeds. Blocking is private — the blocked person is not notified.

Report to us directly: info@rallysphere.com

If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first. In the United States, you can also report to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) at report.cybertip.org or 1-800-843-5678.

How we respond

Reports of child safety concerns are prioritized above all other report categories. When we receive one, we:

  • Review the reported content, account, and any related activity.
  • Remove violating content and permanently terminate the accounts responsible.
  • Preserve relevant records as required for law enforcement.
  • Report apparent child sexual abuse material to NCMEC, and cooperate with law enforcement and other authorities as required by law.

We do not require a report to act. Where we identify violating content or conduct ourselves, we take the same steps.

Legal compliance

RallySphere complies with all applicable child safety laws in the jurisdictions where we operate, including U.S. federal reporting obligations to NCMEC under 18 U.S.C. § 2258A. We respond to valid legal process from law enforcement and regional and national authorities.

Point of contact

Our designated point of contact for child safety and CSAE compliance matters can be reached at info@rallysphere.com. For questions about this policy or any other trust and safety topic, contact info@rallysphere.com.
